What the review covers

Every review answers two separate questions, because a paper can pass the first and still fail the second.

Part one

Do your numbers reproduce?

We write an independent implementation of your analysis from your data and methods section, then compare it with every value you report.

  • Sample sizes and exclusions at each step
  • Estimates, intervals, and p-values in every table
  • Agreement between text, tables, and figures
  • If you share code, whether it does what your methods say

Part two

Do your conclusions hold?

We review whether the analysis supports what the paper claims, and where possible we run the corrected analysis to show what changes.

  • Model specification, adjustment sets, and survey design
  • Variables that belong outside the model, such as mediators
  • Sparse categories and unstable estimates
  • Claims in the abstract that the results don't support

Findings are ranked as major, moderate, or minor, each with a specific recommended edit. The code and outputs behind every finding are available to you on request.

What we need from your data

The full review depends on whether we can get the data you used. You never need to send us data your agreement doesn't allow you to share.

Public or registration-based data

Datasets such as NHANES, DHS, CHARLS, or CDC WONDER. We obtain the data under our own access and run the full two-part review.

Restricted or private data

We review your code and statistics against the manuscript without re-running on the data. Your report will say clearly which checks this covers.

Questions

Who can submit a manuscript?

Only an author of the manuscript. When you submit, you confirm that you're an author. We can't review papers sent by editors or peer reviewers, because most journals don't allow manuscripts under review to be shared.

Is my manuscript kept confidential?

Yes. We don't share or publish anything about an unpublished manuscript. If your paper is published later, we'll only feature its review with your permission.

Your files are deleted 90 days after your last pass.

Do you use AI?

Yes. An AI-assisted pipeline builds the independent implementation and drafts the findings. A person reviews every report before it's sent to you.

How long does a review take?

Most reports arrive within 5 business days of receiving your files. Revision passes are usually faster.

Does a clean report mean my paper will be accepted?

No. The review checks whether your numbers reproduce and whether your analysis supports your conclusions. It doesn't predict what a journal or its reviewers will decide.

What if my submission is very large?

Very large submissions, such as extensive supplements with many models, may need a quote before we start. We'll tell you before any work begins.

What if you can't run the review?

If we can't get the data or can't run the analysis you describe, we'll tell you what we can check instead. If you'd rather not continue, you get a full refund.
