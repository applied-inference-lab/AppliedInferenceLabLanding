What we collect

Information you send us

  • Your manuscript and related files. This includes drafts, supplementary material, code, and any notes you send about your analysis.
  • Your contact details. Your name, email address, and anything else in your emails to us, such as your affiliation and your co-authors' names if they appear in the manuscript.
  • Your authorship confirmation. We ask you to confirm you're an author of each manuscript you submit.

Please don't send us individual-level research data or anything that identifies study participants. When a review needs data, we get public or registration-based datasets ourselves, under our own access.

Information from payment

When you pay, Stripe collects your payment details, email address, and billing location. Stripe shares with us your name, email, billing location, any tax ID you enter, the amount paid, and the last four digits of your card. We use these to match your payment to your submission, calculate tax, and keep accounting records.

Information from this website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. Two services see basic technical information when you load a page:

  • Our web host, [website host], records standard server logs such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.

How we use it

  • To carry out the review you paid for, including revision passes.
  • To communicate with you about your review.
  • To process payments, issue receipts and invoices, and meet tax and accounting obligations.
  • To check the quality of our own reviews. We may re-examine past reports to measure how accurate our findings are.
  • To produce aggregate statistics, such as how often submitted analyses reproduce. Aggregate statistics never identify you, your co-authors, or your manuscript.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to join one.

How AI is involved

Our review process uses AI services from third-party providers to help build the independent implementation of your analysis and to draft findings. A person reviews every report before it's sent to you. These providers process your manuscript only to deliver that service to us, under terms that [do not allow them to use your files to train their models].
